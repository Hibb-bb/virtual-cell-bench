Put a model on the scale

Three ways in, one response format. For version 0.1 the maintainers run the pipeline: open a pull request against the repository or write to us, and the model is placed with item difficulties anchored and published with its ability and standard error.

Route 1 · embeddings

  • One matrix per panel, cells × dimensions, every cell (training and test) in the panel's cell order.
  • .npy, or .h5ad with the embedding in obsm.
  • Panels ship as .h5ad with raw counts, the split column and strata. Test labels are included: the probe is ours and the split is by whole donors, studies or tissues.
  • State which cells the model saw in pretraining.

The scVI respondents took this route: scripts/extract_scvi_embedding.py, then scripts/append_repr_responses.py.

Route 2 · predictions

  • One matrix per screen and split, held-out conditions × genes, in the screen's gene order.
  • Rows follow the published fold assignment (fold_group in the items table).
  • Train on the training folds and all control cells; never on the held-out conditions.
  • Scored with the fixed metric and pass rule.

scGen and the scVI counterfactual took this route through scripts/append_pert_scores.py.

Route 3 · response table

If you reproduce the probe or the pass rule yourself, send the table both components share:

item_id                  respondent_id  k  n
repr_pool_v0::cell_0001  my_model_v1    1  1
tahoe100m::drugX::ctx=A549::dose=0.05  my_model_v1  0  1
  • k passes out of n trials: cell items have n = 1, stratum items the stratum size.
  • Item identifiers must match the published items tables exactly.
  • Tables are checked against the items we hold out before placement.

Contamination register

For every learned model, declare per source whether it was in pretraining, in fine-tuning, or clean. Most entries for current foundation models will not be clean, and that is fine: the register is published next to the ability and is what makes it interpretable.

House rules

  • Respondent names are stable across every run and version.
  • Published items are never modified; a fix produces a new panel or screen and a new version.
  • Classical respondents and floors are refit alongside every submission.
  • Abilities from different versions are never compared without saying so.
  • Submissions that only tie the baseline are flagged as misfit, not ranked.

Where a new source helps most

  • Representation items above +2 logits that are not label noise: treatment or disease state, at pseudobulk resolution.
  • Perturbation screens with chemical descriptors, so unseen-compound prediction becomes learnable.
  • A cytokine to gene mapping for the Parse screen.
  • Foundation-model embeddings and predictions with a complete contamination declaration.
